While unemployed in Finland, the artist was entitled to financial support through the country’s social democratic welfare system. To receive monthly benefits, he was required to submit a form to the unemployment office confirming his unemployment status. The word *Työtön* (Finnish for “unemployed”) twenty times on these forms became his only work during that period of his life in Finland. This repetitive administrative ritual, together with the gradual shift toward neoliberal policies and changes to the welfare system, became the starting point for *Työtön*. The work expresses this experience through the word “unemployed” burning into stone, transforming a temporary bureaucratic declaration into a permanent inscription.
