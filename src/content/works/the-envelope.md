As part of the project “Ruse - past and future, urban dreams and visions,” Fatmir Mustafa Karllo presents the Envelope, a performative and process-based work exploring the invisible layers of history, identity, and urban psychology embedded in public spaces. During a series of walks through Ruse, Karllo collected dust from national buildings, statues, and flags. This material was carefully gathered and mixed with dust previously collected in Prishtina, then transferred onto the canvas with a gentle puff of air.
