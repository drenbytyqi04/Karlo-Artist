*I’m Not Drunk* (2011) is a sound recording of an intoxicated man repeatedly stating, “I’m not drunk.” His voice, intonation, and rhythm of speech change throughout the recording. Following the recording, the artist approached Nokia with a request to provide a mobile phone for exhibiting the work.

Presented in the same space: [*Che & S*](/works/che-and-s/) (2011).
