On February 10, 2007, during a protest organized by the Vetëvendosja movement in Kosovo, Karllo, along with the friendly support of Ron Qena, offered hot tea with lemon to both demonstrators and the police in an attempt to alleviate tensions and to calm the spirits. Regrettably, the protest took a violent turn when a Romanian police unit fired rubber bullets, resulting in the tragic deaths of two protesters and numerous injuries. Perhaps this bitter experience with ‘Tea Mediation’ motivated Karllo to level charges of plundering the future of young people and his country.
