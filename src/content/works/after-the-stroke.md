Per Karllo: “As an artist you must suffer a stroke, or some kind of a shock, or a trauma, in order to create a new vision. The process of invention is traumatic. It is as if you are chopping at your old values to make space for something new.” And this is not exclusive to art. Building new futures and systems — especially those found in weird spaces, like in between two layers — is a traumatic event.
