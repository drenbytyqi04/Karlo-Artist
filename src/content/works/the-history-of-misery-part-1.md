Van de Graaff generator: its metallic sphere invites you to touch it. The device can produce high voltages relative to the environment, which in some applications can discharge into lightning or, as in this case, simply make people’s hair stand on end. In the white layer of paint on the walls of the entrance, there are barely visible carvings showing copies of names, dates, or messages left by prisoners in their lightless cell of the local prison during the so-called Alexander Rankovic period.
