*120 min MUSE/UM* was a two-hour public art event held in central Helsinki in May 2012 as part of a summer class organized by Outi Turpeinen in collaboration with the Ateneum Art Museum. The event aimed to transform public space into a living museum, questioning the conventions and objectivity of traditional museums.

During the event, Fatmir Mustafa Karllo transported oversized cardboard cutouts of male models from the Post Museum to the Ateneum Art Museum. As the artist carried the figures through the streets of Helsinki, the intervention blurred the boundaries between art and everyday life, provoking confusion, curiosity, and laughter among passersby.
