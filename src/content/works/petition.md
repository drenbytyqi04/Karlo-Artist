In his installation titled “Petition” the artist Fatmir Mustafa presents us with the problematics facing the immigrant, refugee living in a host country, loss of dignity and set power relations. On the literal surface he constructs an outline of an envelope made out of thick rope slightly elevated at one corner, at the other corner the rope continues on the floor as if to be followed then rises sharply vertically up forcing the viewer to look up, to find where the rope has its start… from the open zipper (fly) of trousers hung at the ceiling of the space. The images of the rope/envelope and trousers with an open fly/rope suggest frustration of losing human dignity in the processes and bureaucratism of host countries. The piece questions the power relations between the “arrived” and the “welcoming”, the artist purposefully placed very small drawings attached to the envelope depicting a priest, a lawyer and imam, the drawings are imperceptible from afar suggesting tastefully that real power is unseen.
