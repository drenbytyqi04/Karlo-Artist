Here the innocent notion of a picnic is redefined as a consumerist sport, which co-opts nature for its ends. The pretzels are the same as the ones we used to buy as kids the night before our school-trip picnics. Yet this infantile bliss is transmuted and corrupted into a more traumatic and adult variant stuffed with money. The glass wall — which in itself becomes one of those mysterious spaces between two realities — separates the spectator, but also enables the ones with the necessary privileges to access the restricted area on the “inside.” This makes the artwork seem like a life-size diorama much like the ones found in some Natural History Museum, as if this “novel” picnic is preserved for the viewers from a distant and utopian future.
