*The Last Witness* addresses Kosovo’s recent history — the violence and the atrocities that were suffered during the conflict and remain since. In the center of the work stands an old wooden coffin, installed vertically. Fatmir Mustafa Karllo received it from the local mosque “Xh. Fusha e Pajtimit”, where it had been in use for 15 years, and bought a new one to replace it. This coffin was used for the burials of many people in post-war Kosovo, representing some suspicious deaths related to the war. Inside the coffin, he placed a sculpture — a silhouette of a human covered with a white sheet. This ghostly witness is bringing a new kind of testimony that is not looking at the past from the present, but rather bringing it to the present as an object.
