*Krapula* is a ready-made installation-sculpture consisting of a black leather wallet placed inside an official KELA envelope, issued by Finland’s Social Insurance Institution. Through these two objects, the work explores the relationship between the individual and the state, as well as the uncertainty surrounding changes in the social welfare system.
